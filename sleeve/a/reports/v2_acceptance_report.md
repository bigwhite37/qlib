## 8. What bounds the result

1. **Trading cost dominates.** The recorded fills show 23-35x NAV of traded notional per year. At the 100k account scale the average fill is 2.5-5k CNY, so the 5 CNY minimum commission alone costs 10-33bp per fill: total fees run 6-11% of NAV per year. The same strategy at a 1M account pays 3.8%/yr instead (v2_band_c1m), which shows the drag is a scale effect, not a strategy defect.
2. **Selection alpha is modest.** Cross-sectional rank IC against 20-day forward returns is about -0.07 for volatility, -0.05 for liquidity and -0.05 for medium-term reversal. The rolling model's top decile beats the universe by only about +0.45pp per trade; the causal rule composite (low volatility + low liquidity + distance below MA60) is stronger than the model alone, and a 50/50 blend of the two is stronger than either.
3. **The frozen exit policy matters more than the entry.** Under the same ranker, an 8% stop costs about 4x the net alpha per holding day compared with no stop and a 60-day cap (0.0075 vs 0.031 x100 per day), because the stop sells into volatility rather than into decay.
4. **The account arithmetic then follows.** CAGR is approximately information ratio times the volatility budget. The best configuration found reaches IR 0.83 (10.3% CAGR at 12.4% vol). Reaching 25% CAGR inside a 10% volatility cap requires IR 2.5, i.e. about three times the best value measured here, together with a 70% win rate.
5. **The win rate is a policy choice, not free alpha.** Unconditioned, the chosen policy wins 55% of round trips; shrinking the profit target toward 2% and lengthening the hold pushes the unconditioned win rate to 62%, but expectancy stays between -0.5% and -0.8% per trade, so the higher hit rate is bought with tail risk rather than created.
6. **Drawdown is the binding risk constraint.** The best run's -22.7% comes almost entirely from the February-July 2024 small-cap episode; the drawdown-to-volatility ratio is about 1.8. Holding MDD at -10% therefore caps realized volatility near 5.5% and CAGR near 4-5%.

### The evidence that this is the ceiling of this data set

Five stage-1 rounds closed **twelve** mechanisms, every one with a control that reproduced the
headline bit for bit: third-opinion consensus (13 partners), sub-book ensembling over 511 recorded
runs (best combination +0.086 IR; reaching 2.5x needs a pairwise correlation of 0.16 against a
median of 0.836), the liquidity measure and its window, portfolio breadth, the intraday exit
trigger, the absolute entry gate (a verified no-op), the ranking-triggered exit, inverse-vol
re-weighting, hard consensus, and three forms of entry patience. Together with the earlier rounds -
five falsifications of "a better cross-sectional signal improves the account", the whole exit-policy
search, the risk layer, and the drawdown work - the measured information ratio has stayed in the
band 0.90-1.00 for twelve consecutive independent interventions.

The one intervention that ever moved it is the exit-policy redesign of rounds 23-24 (profit target
3.5% -> 7.5%, hold cap 45 -> 120 days), worth +2.5pp of CAGR and +8pp of win rate at equal risk.
Everything else is a negative, and the entry side in particular behaves like a strong local optimum
that no re-ranking, re-weighting, re-gating or re-timing rule can escape.



## 12. Final stage statement (eight stage-1 rounds)

**Neither stage 1 nor stage 2 was reached, and stage 3 is not reachable within the stated
constraints.** The measured seed-averaged information ratio is **0.948** at the compliant exposure
and **0.945** at the return end, against stage 1's 1.30, stage 2's 1.60 and the contract's 2.50.
Fifteen design mechanisms were implemented and tested across eight rounds, every one with a control
that reproduced the incumbent bit for bit, and none moved the information ratio outside the
**0.94-1.00** band.

What the contract does deliver today, on all nine model seeds:

* **win rate 74.8-80.5%** (requirement >= 70%) - met on 9 of 9 seeds;
* **volatility 9.67%** (requirement <= 10%) - met on 9 of 9 seeds;
* both holding-frequency floors - met on 9 of 9 seeds;
* **4 of 7 contract checks on every seed** at the compliant point, 3 of 7 at the return end;
* CAGR 9.16% (compliant) / 13.76% (return end), positive in every calendar year, against the 25%
  requirement.

The gap is a factor of 2.75 in information ratio. It is not a drawdown-mechanics problem (the
drawdown-to-volatility ratio co-moves with IR at -0.55 across 503 runs), not a leverage problem (the
contract caps volatility), not an execution problem (the intraday-trigger and scale-out variants are
both worse), and not a search-effort problem (the entry side behaves as a strong local optimum that
no re-ranking, re-weighting, re-gating, re-timing, re-universing or re-training rule escapes).

The only remaining routes are outside the current constraints:

1. **new data** - stock-level intraday bars for 2019-2026 (to model fills and intraday targets
   directly) or a flow/fundamental block with full history (northbound holdings, margin balances,
   industry membership) to supply a genuinely complementary second opinion; or
2. **a changed objective** - accepting ~9% CAGR at 10% volatility (compliant), ~14% at 14.6%
   volatility (return end), or relaxing the volatility and drawdown ceilings so that the return end
   becomes the headline.
